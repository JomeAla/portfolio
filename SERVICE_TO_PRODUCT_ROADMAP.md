## Problem Statement

**Current Challenges:**
- Services require extensive time investment
- Revenue is linear (1 hour = 1 client)
- Limited scalability
- High client acquisition cost
- No automated marketing funnels

**Solution:**
Convert services to digital products that can:
- Be sold infinitely
- Generate leads automatically
- Create upsell opportunities
- Build authority

## PRIORITY RECOMMENDATIONS

### Priority 1: Website Audit Service (₦5,000)
**Why it's the best first product:**
- ✅ Low barrier to entry for buyers
- ✅ Everyone with a website needs an audit
- ✅ Easy to deliver (checklist + video)
- ✅ Converts to full projects 3x better than cold outreach
- ✅ Creates instant authority

**Funnel:**
```
Free Guide: "5 Critical Website Mistakes" 
    ↓
₦5,000: Website Audit Kit + 20-page report
    ↓
Upsell: Fix the issues = ₦250k-500k full project
```

**Expected ROI:** 1 in 10 buyers upgrade (10% conversion)

---

### Priority 2: WordPress Starter Kit (₦12,000)
**Why:**
- Every Nigerian SME needs a website
- High demand + low competition in Nigeria
- Easy delivery
- Upsells to full development work

**Funnel:**
```
Free: WordPress Setup Guide
    ↓
₦12,000: WordPress Starter Kit (themes + plugins + guide)
    ↓
Upsell: Custom development or maintenance
```

---

### Priority 3: Business Automation Playbook (₦18,000)
**Why:**
- Nigerian businesses are overwhelmed with manual work
- WhatsApp Business is exploding in Nigeria
- Low competition in this niche

**Content:**
- WhatsApp automation templates
- Email sequence templates
- CRM setup guide
- Automation checklist
