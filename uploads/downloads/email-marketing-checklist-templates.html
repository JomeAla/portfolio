✅ Email Marketing Checklist

Define your target audience
Who are you sending to? What are their pain points, desires, and goals?
Set clear email goals
What action do you want them to take? (Open, Click, Reply, Buy)
Clean your email list
Remove bounces, unopens, and spam traps. Aim for 90%+ list hygiene.
Write compelling subject lines (under 50 chars)
Test curiosity, urgency, or personalization. A/B test regularly.
Personalize with recipient's name
Use {name} or first name. Connection builds trust.
Start with a strong hook (first line visible)
Preview text is your first impression. Make it count.
Keep emails short (under 200 words)
One idea per email. Use scannable formatting.
Include ONE clear call-to-action
One link or button. More = confusion.
Add professional signature
Photo, title, and social links build credibility.
Test on mobile (60%+ opens)
Short paragraphs, large buttons, responsive design.
Set up automation for key triggers
Welcome, cart abandonment, re-engagement sequences.
Track metrics weekly
Open rate, click rate, reply rate, conversions.
