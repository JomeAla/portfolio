4. Google Business Profile Setup Guide

Google Business Profile Checklist
STEP 1: Create Profile - Go to business.google.com - Click "Manage now" - Enter business name EXACTLY as you want it - Choose category (be specific!) STEP 2: Add Location - Enter full address - Mark exact location on map - Set service area STEP 3: Business Info - Phone: +234[Phone] - Website: [Link] - Hours: Mon-Sat [X]am-[X]pm - Payment: Cash, Transfer STEP 4: Add Photos - Logo/Brand image - Interior photo - Products photo - Team photo STEP 5: Get Reviews - Share direct link - Respond to ALL reviews PRO TIP: Ask happy customers on the spot! "Would you mind leaving us a quick review?"
