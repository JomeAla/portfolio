Content Categories Explained

  • Educational: Teach concepts and ideas
  • How-To: Step-by-step tutorials
  • Case Studies: Real examples with results
  • Problem-Solve: Address pain points
  • Comparison: Help decisions
  • Resource: Tools, templates, checklists
  • Community: Engage your audience

Tips for Content Success

  1. Consistency beats perfection: Post regularly, even if not perfect
  2. Repurpose content: Turn blog posts into social posts, emails, videos
  3. Update old posts: Refresh content every 6 months
  4. Engage with comments: Reply to every comment
  5. Promote your posts: Share on social media and email
