📧 Welcome Email Sequence Templates

Use these templates to welcome new subscribers and set the tone for your brand.

Email #1: The Welcome Email
Send: Immediately after signup

Subject Lines (choose one):

  • Welcome to {{business_name}}!
  • You're in! Here's what to do next...
  • Thanks for joining {{business_name}}!

Email Body:

Hi {{first_name}}, Welcome to {{business_name}}! I'm {{your_name}}, and I'm thrilled you joined us. You're now part of a community of {{audience_type}} who receive exclusive tips, resources, and insights. Here's what you can expect: 📚 Weekly tips on {{topic}} 🎁 Exclusive resources (coming soon!) 💡 Industry insights and trends To get started, I'd love for you to: [Button: Download Your Free Guide / Visit Our Website] If you have any questions, just reply to this email. I'm here to help! Looking forward to {{action}}, {{your_name}} Founder, {{business_name}}

Email #4: Ask for Introduction
Send: 7 days after signup

Subject Lines:

  • A quick question for you, {{first_name}}
  • Who do you know that needs {{topic}}?
  • Help me help more people like you

Email Body:

Hi {{first_name}}, I hope you're finding our emails valuable! I have a small favor to ask... You signed up because you're interested in {{topic}}. I bet you know a few people who could benefit from this too. Would you mind introducing me to: - A friend who runs a business - A colleague who handles marketing - Someone you know who struggles with {{problem}} Just forward this email or paste their name in your reply, and I'll take it from there. As a thank you, I'd like to offer you {{incentive}} (just mention their name). Thanks for your support! {{your_name}} P.S. No pressure at all. But if you do know someone, I'd really appreciate the intro!

Subject Lines:

  • Last chance to join {{product_name}}

Email Body:

Hi {{first_name}}, I wanted to let you know that {{product_name}} is now open! This is perfect for {{target_audience}} who want to {{transformation}}. Here's what you'll get: 🎯 {{benefit_1}} 🎯 {{benefit_2}} 🎯 {{benefit_3}} Plus, as a subscriber, you get {{subscriber_bonus}}. [Button: Join {{product_name}}] This offer is open until {{deadline}}, so don't wait! If you have questions, just reply. I'd love to help you get started. Talk soon, {{your_name}} P.S. Not ready to commit? No problem - you can stay subscribed for free tips. But this offer won't come again soon!

How to Customize These Templates

Replace all {{placeholders}} with your specific values:

  • {{business_name}} - Your company name
  • {{first_name}} - Subscriber's first name
  • {{your_name}} - Your name
  • {{topic}} - Your main topic/niche
  • {{target_audience}} - Who you help
  • {{lead_magnet}} - Your free giveaway
  • {{product_name}} - Your main product/service
